Helping consumer brands adopt AI, and keep using it

Pixis · e-commerce and consumer brands including PharmEasy, Man Matters, Gynoveda and Lakmé
The challenge

Advertisers were signed up to an AI platform for their online campaigns, but few used it day to day. The gap was trust and understanding, not the product.

What I did

Helped e-commerce brands such as PharmEasy, Man Matters and Gynoveda adopt the platform, then worked with their marketing teams to optimise live Meta and Google campaigns on it, diagnosing what was underperforming and rebuilding account structure, audiences and creative. Rebuilt onboarding, in-product guidance, lifecycle messages and usage-triggered outreach. Fed what customers struggled with back to Product and Engineering.

The outcome

Adoption rose from under 5% to 90% in 12 months, and customer feedback shaped features and naming that shipped to production.
